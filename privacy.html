Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

1. Who Operates Accord Archive

Accord Media House ("Accord", "we", "our") operates the Accord Digital Library & Historical Archive at accordarchives.com. Accord is a faith-centered Christian digital library and fellowship community committed to maintaining a safe, respectful environment. For all privacy, legal, or data inquiries, contact us at derrick@accordofficial.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Data: When you create an account or sign in via Google, we collect your name, email address, unique @handle, minimum-age and Terms acceptance timestamp, and optional ministry/bio details.
  • Approximate Location & Audience Signals (With Your Consent): If you accept non-essential cookies/analytics in our Cookie Preferences banner, we use approximate location (country, region/state, and city) derived from standard network/edge headers (without requesting precise browser GPS permissions), a pseudonymous browser session key (accord_session_key), device type, and page engagement signals to measure aggregated advertising reach and deliver relevant regional promotions.
  • Essential Storage & Regional Pricing: Even if you choose "Essential Only" in Cookie Preferences, we use strictly necessary authentication storage and temporary session-only country lookup (accord_geo) so the site can display prices in your local currency without persistent cross-session tracking.
  • Contributions & Transactions: Information you provide when submitting manuscripts, posts, comments, moderation reports, paid promotions, book purchases, or author payout requests.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate accounts, synchronize saved bookmarks, and deliver real-time chat and notifications.
  • To display relevant regional promotions and measure aggregated performance (impressions and clicks) when you consent to audience signals.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service, protect community and child safety, investigate flagged content, track policy violations, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To process digital book purchases, paid promotions, and author royalty withdrawals via Paystack.

4. Third-Party Embedded Media

Accord Archive does not directly host songs, sermons, or videos on our own media servers; instead, these items are linked as third-party embeds (such as YouTube, Spotify, or external media providers). When you view or play an embedded player, the third-party provider may receive your IP address, browser/device information, and set its own cookies in accordance with its own privacy policy.

5. Data Storage, Subprocessors & International Transfers

Authentication and database records are hosted on Supabase, payments and payouts are processed via Paystack, transactional emails are delivered via Resend, and web hosting/edge routing is provided by Vercel. Because our users and infrastructure span multiple countries, your data may be transferred to and processed in jurisdictions outside your home country subject to appropriate contractual safeguards. We do not sell your personal contact details to third-party data brokers.

6. Children's Privacy & Minimum Age

Accord Archive is intended for users aged 13 and older (or 16 and older where required by local law, such as in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child under the minimum age has created an account, please contact derrick@accordofficial.com so we can promptly remove the account.

7. Data Retention & Your Privacy Rights

We retain account and transaction records for as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with legal, tax, and safety obligations. Depending on your location (including Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), the EU/UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent for non-essential audience signals at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in the footer.

To submit a data access, correction, or account deletion request, contact us at derrick@accordofficial.com. We aim to respond to all verified privacy requests within 30 days.
