Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Accord Media House ("Accord", "we", "our") operates the Accord Digital Library & Historical Archive at accordarchives.com. Browsing and downloading public archives requires no account. If you voluntarily create an account or sign in via Google OAuth, we collect your name, email address, and profile identifier solely to authenticate your session and sync your saved reading list.

If you submit a historical manuscript through our contribution form, we collect the name, email address, and manuscript details you provide for editorial review.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate optional reader accounts and synchronize bookmarked publications across devices.
  • To communicate with contributors regarding submitted historical manuscripts.
  • To maintain platform security, prevent abuse, and analyze anonymized page performance.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Authentication and database records are securely hosted on Supabase, transactional emails are delivered via Resend, and web hosting is provided by Vercel. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may sign out at any time or request deletion of your account and associated reading list data by contacting us at derrick@accordofficial.com.
