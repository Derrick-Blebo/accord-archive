Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Accord Media House ("Accord", "we", "our") operates the Accord Digital Library & Historical Archive at accordarchives.com. We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Profile Data: When you create an account or sign in via Google, we collect your name, email address, unique @handle, Terms acceptance timestamp, and optional ministry/bio details.
  • Approximate Location & Audience Signals: To deliver relevant regional promotions and measure advertising reach, we collect approximate location (country, region/state, and city) derived from standard network/edge headers (without requesting precise browser GPS permissions), along with device type and page engagement signals.
  • Contributions & Transactions: Information you provide when submitting manuscripts, posts, comments, moderation reports, paid promotions, book purchases, or author payout requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate accounts, synchronize saved bookmarks, and deliver real-time chat and notifications.
  • To target, display, and measure aggregated performance (impressions and clicks) of ad banners and paid promotions based on approximate location and interests.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service, investigate flagged content, track policy violations, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To process digital book purchases, paid promotions, and author royalty withdrawals via Paystack.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Authentication and database records are hosted on Supabase, payments and payouts are processed via Paystack, transactional emails are delivered via Resend, and web hosting/edge routing is provided by Vercel. We do not sell your personal contact details to third-party data brokers.

4. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may update your profile information or sign out at any time. To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us at derrick@accordofficial.com.
